Where she works

North Lucknow ke teen corridor

Everything Deepika shows sits on one of these three roads. Different budgets, different reasons to buy.

Jankipuram Extension

The fastest-filling residential pocket of the three. Plots and villas sit alongside each other, schools and daily markets are already running, and construction on neighbouring streets is visible — useful if you want to see a locality actually forming rather than promised.
