01Who this policy covers

DG Realty Lucknow is an independent real-estate advisory represented by Deepika Gupta. We help buyers look at residential plots, villas and row houses, and farmhouses in Jankipuram Extension, Sitapur Road and Kursi Road, Lucknow.

This policy applies to this website and to enquiries that reach us through it — including the enquiry form, the WhatsApp buttons and the call buttons. Where the words “we”, “us” and “our” appear below, they mean DG Realty Lucknow.

02What information we collect

We only ask for what is needed to understand your requirement and get back to you. When you send an enquiry, this is what we collect:

Name
So we know who we are speaking with.
Phone number
The number we call or message you on. This is usually your WhatsApp number too.
Email address Optional
Only if you choose to give one — for sending details in writing.
Property type
Whether you are looking at a plot, a villa or row house, or a farmhouse.
Preferred area
The locality you are interested in, such as Jankipuram Extension, Sitapur Road or Kursi Road.
Budget range
So that the options we show you are worth your time.
Buying or site-visit timeline
When you are hoping to visit or to buy, so we can plan around it.
Your message Optional
Anything else you want to tell us about what you are looking for.
Your city Optional
Helpful when you are buying in Lucknow from another city.

Two further things are recorded automatically, and you should know about them:

  • Where the enquiry came from. If you arrived through an advertisement or a shared link, the campaign reference attached to that link is saved along with your enquiry. It tells us which advert you responded to. It does not identify you personally.
  • A copy saved in your own browser. Your last enquiry is stored on your own device so the page can fill in your details and prepare your WhatsApp message. It stays on your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

We do not ask for identity documents, PAN or Aadhaar numbers, bank or payment details through this website. No payments are collected here.

03Why we collect it

Your details are used for three things, and nothing else:

  • Responding to your property enquiry — getting back to you about what you asked for.
  • Sharing options that match your requirement — property type, area and budget together tell us what is worth sending you.
  • Coordinating site visits — agreeing a date and time, and giving you directions and access details.

We do not add you to a bulk marketing list, and we do not use your details for anything unrelated to the enquiry you made.

04How we contact you

We reach out by phone call and WhatsApp on the number you give us. WhatsApp is usually fastest. If you have shared an email address, we may also send details in writing to it.

Calls are normally made between 10:00 and 19:00 IST. You can tell us at any time to use one channel only, to contact you at a different time, or to stop contacting you altogether — just say so on WhatsApp or on a call, and it will be respected.

Messages sent over WhatsApp also pass through WhatsApp itself, which handles them under its own terms and privacy policy.

05We do not sell your information

Your details are never sold, rented, traded or passed to data brokers, lead-buying networks or third-party marketing lists. We do not exchange enquiry lists with other agents or brokers.

The only time your name and phone number would go to anyone else is when it is needed to arrange something you have asked for — for example, giving your name at a site gate so that a visit can go ahead. That is done for that visit alone, and with your agreement. We may also have to disclose information if the law requires it.

06How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry while we are helping you, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us about the same requirement. When it is no longer needed, or when you ask us to remove it, it is deleted.

The copy saved in your own browser is under your control and is removed when you clear your browser data.

07Correcting or deleting your information

You can ask us at any time to correct something that is wrong, or to delete your details entirely. There is no form to fill in and no charge.

  • Send a message on WhatsApp to +91 96484 10410, call the same number, or email deepikagupta@gmail.com.
  • Say what you want done — “please correct my budget” or “please delete my details”. Sending it from the phone number or email you originally used helps us find your enquiry quickly.
  • We will action it and confirm, normally within seven days of receiving the request.

Asking us to delete your details will end any ongoing conversation about a property, since we will no longer have a way to reach you. You are welcome to enquire again later.

08Verifying property information

Anything shown on this website or shared in conversation — availability, prices, plot and built-up sizes, layouts, photographs, maps and approval status — is indicative and can change without notice. It is shared to help you shortlist, not as a formal offer.

Please verify everything independently before you commit money. Check the title and chain of ownership, the layout and building approvals, encumbrance and tax position, measured area and boundaries, and every document, with your own advocate and with the relevant authorities.

Do not rely only on what is presented here. Nothing on this website is a guarantee of returns, appreciation, resale value, loan or approval of any kind, and nothing here forms a contract. No payments are collected on this website.
